Hourhand / For therapists

A booking link that never puts two clients in the same hour

A double-booking is worse in a therapy practice than anywhere else. Hourhand checks every calendar you connect and stops offering times the moment it can’t.

What gets in the way

Clinic and private calendars

You work across a clinic diary and your own, and one of them is always out of date.

Back-to-back sessions

Without a buffer, notes and a breath between clients disappear.

Late cancellations

A cancellation an hour before is an empty slot you can’t refill.

How Hourhand handles it

Pauses instead of guessing

Every calendar has a sync light. If the clinic calendar can’t be checked, your page stops offering times and we email you.

Breaks between sessions

Add time before and after each session so nobody books into your note-writing time.

Cancellation cut-off

Set how late clients can cancel or move a session from their email.

Private notes

Keep private notes and each client’s session history on the booking, visible only to you.

Hidden session types

Offer intake or couples sessions by direct link only, so they don’t appear on your public page.
