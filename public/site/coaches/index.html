What gets in the way

Clients in other time zones

You do the maths for every international call and still get it wrong once a month.

No-shows on free calls

Discovery calls get booked and forgotten, and the slot was one a paying client wanted.

Programmes are admin

An 8-week programme means eight bookings, eight reminders and eight chances for a clash.

How Hourhand handles it

Times in their zone

Each guest sees your free times in their own time zone, with a 12-hour or 24-hour clock. You never convert a time again.

Hidden discovery calls

Keep a free 15-minute call off your public page and send its link only to people you’ve qualified.

Weekly repeats

Book a client into the same slot every week for up to 12 weeks in one go. Each session gets its own reminders.

Paid at booking

Charge for sessions or sell packages through your own Razorpay account, with refundable no-show deposits. Switching on soon.

An assistant on Duo

Add someone to run your bookings at no extra cost. Duo is one flat price for two people.
