Two things a booking tool should never do

1

Say you’re free when you’re not

A calendar quietly stops syncing, a client books over another session, and you only find out when two people turn up.

2

Go quiet when it breaks

You get a bot or a help article instead of an answer. And leaving turns out to be harder than joining was.

Live in five minutes

Three steps, then your link does the scheduling.

  1. Connect your calendar

    Pick the calendars to check for clashes. Each one gets its own sync light showing when it was last checked.

  2. Share your link

    Put it in your Instagram bio, your WhatsApp messages or your website. Guests see only your free times.

  3. Show up

    The booking lands in your calendar with a Google Meet link. Your guest gets a confirmation and reminders.

Every guest books in their own time and language

  • Their own time zone, with a 12-hour or 24-hour clock. A client in London sees London time.
  • Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and English booking pages
  • Your own questions on the form, and guests can add others to the booking
  • Built for phones, where most of your clients will tap the link

Emails that do the chasing

Every email carries a calendar file your guest adds in one tap.

  1. On bookingConfirmation to your guest, notice to you
  2. 24 hours beforeReminder with the time in their zone
  3. 1 hour beforeReminder with the Google Meet link
  4. If it movesGuests reschedule or cancel from their email
  5. If you cancelThey get your three nearest free times

Guests can also tap “I’m running late” and tell you 5 to 30 minutes ahead, so you’re not left waiting.

Questions

Something else? Write to support@hourhand.cloud and a person will reply.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes, for one person, for good. You get two calendars checked for clashes, booking emails with calendar files and Google Meet links. No card is needed to start.

What happens if my calendar disconnects?

Your booking page stops offering times straight away, so nobody can book over a session. We email you a link to reconnect, and your times come back as soon as the calendar can be checked again.

Which calendars does Hourhand work with?

Google Calendar today. Outlook and iCloud calendars, and Microsoft Teams links, are switching on next.

Can my clients book in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu or Kannada?

Yes. Your booking page works in those four languages and English, and every guest sees times in their own time zone.

How do payments work?

You connect your own Razorpay account and clients pay you directly, including by UPI. Hourhand never holds your money and takes no percentage of it. Payments are switching on soon on the Solo and Duo plans.

Can I use Hourhand with clients outside India?

Yes. Guests anywhere see your free times in their own time zone, with a 12-hour or 24-hour clock, and you can pay for your plan in US dollars.

Does a real person answer support?

Yes. Write to support@hourhand.cloud and a person replies within one working day, on every plan, including Free.

How do I cancel?

One tap in Settings. We email you 7 days before every renewal, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. You can also delete your account and its data from Settings.
