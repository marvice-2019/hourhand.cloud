Cancellation and refunds

Effective 23 October 2026

Cancelling your plan

Cancel any time in one tap from Settings. Your Solo or Duo plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, then your account moves to Free. Your booking page, bookings and data stay in place.

Refunds

  • We do not refund the unused part of a monthly or yearly period after you cancel. We remind you by email 7 days before every renewal so you can cancel in time.
  • If you were charged twice, or charged after you cancelled, we refund the full amount.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

To ask for a refund, email support@hourhand.cloud from your account email with the date and amount of the charge.

Payments your clients make to you

Session fees, packages and deposits your clients pay go to your own Razorpay account. Refunds to clients are made by you, or automatically when a refundable no-show deposit is returned after a session.
